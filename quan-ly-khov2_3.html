Bam Nhan la don vao so kho
Don ban le vao tab Don le, phieu chao hang vao tab Nhap/Xuat trang thai Xuat. Hang ve kho khong mang theo tien — doanh thu chi dem o so hoa don.
Ton kho
▼
Ton = Nhap − Xuat kho − Ban le, cong don toan bo lich su Sheet kho + phieu tren may chua day (giong cong thuc bao cao). Hang giao mang di / mang ve khong tru vao ton. Neu so ton khong khop thuc te, kha nang Sheet thieu du lieu giai doan cu - noi em them chuc nang chot kiem ke.
So khop kho - hoa don
So sanh Mang di − Mang ve (so kho) voi Giao + Tang − Thu hoi (hoa don NV giao). Doi ngang hang khong tinh vao lech. NV chua nhap hoa don thi hien cho HD, chua tinh la lech. Nguon: he van don, lam moi moi 5 phut.
